# Grammar
[[Nouns]] describes how nouns, which represent objects and concepts, are used and their endings.
[[Verbs]] describes how verbs, which represent actions, events, processes, etc., are used and their endings.
[[Adjectives]] describes how adjectives, which describe other words closer, are used and their endings.
[[Pronouns]] describes what pronouns, which refer to things like nouns, exist and how they are used.
[[Particles]] describes what particles, other special words, exist and how they are used.
[[Morph]] describes how words may change their type, even after having partially describes as another type.
[[SentenceStructures]] describes how sentences are formed.
